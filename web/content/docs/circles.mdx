<PlainWords>

A circle is a group of 3 to 20 people who each put in the same amount every month. Each month, one person receives the whole pot. By the end, everyone has had exactly one turn.

Someone creates the circle and shares an invite link. People join with Face ID and pay a small deposit. When the last seat fills, the first month begins.

Turns can go in a fixed order, or members can bid for an early turn by offering a discount.

</PlainWords>

## Lifecycle

<Mermaid
  title="The life of a circle"
  chart={`

    Payout --> Collect: next round
  }

  ACTIVE --> COMPLETED: last round paid
  Settlement --> COMPLETED: repayments collected
  COMPLETED --> [*]: members claim

1. **Create.** `CircleFactory.createCircle(params, fixedOrder, inviteHash)` deploys an EIP-1167 clone of `Circle` and registers it. The invite link carries a secret whose hash is `inviteHash`.
2. **Join.** `join(inviteSecret, displayCurrency)` pulls the entry deposit. In the app, joining is a single Face ID prompt that also approves AUSD and grants auto-pay (see [Accounts](/docs/accounts)).
3. **Start.** When the n-th member joins, `CircleStarted(startTime, members)` fires and round 1 begins.
4. **Rounds.** Each round runs from `roundStart`:
   - `collect()` is open from `roundStart`, and can be retried until the round ends.
   - In auction circles, sealed bids are accepted during the bid window (see [Bidding](/docs/bidding)).
   - `payout()` runs once every active member has **paid** or been **covered**, meaning `markDefault` ran after the grace period and the shortfall was filled.
5. **Settlement.** Only if a member was ejected: extra slots collect repayments (see [Safety](/docs/safety#ejection)).
6. **Complete.** `CircleCompleted` fires. Members `claim()` collateral, deposits, unused credit and their share of the reserve.

A late payer delays the payout by at most `gracePeriod`. They never shrink the pot.

## Parameters

| Parameter | Meaning | Limits and defaults |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `n` | Number of members, and so the number of rounds | 3 to `maxMembers` (10 on the mainnet beta) |
| `contribution` | C, what each member pays per round, in AUSD base units | ≤ `maxContribution` (25 AUSD on the mainnet beta) |
| `period` | Seconds between rounds | Monthly in real circles, 300 s in the demo |
| `mode` | `FIXED_ORDER` or `AUCTION` | |
| `maxDiscountBps` | Largest discount a bid may offer | ≤ 5000 (50%) |
| `bidWindow` | How long bidding stays open each round | Less than `period`; 120 s in the demo |
| `gracePeriod` | How long after collection before a miss can be covered | `bidWindow + gracePeriod` less than `period`; 60 s in the demo |
| `entryDeposit` | Paid at join, covers a first miss before winning | Defaults to C |
| `reserveBps` | Share of each discount kept in the protection reserve | Default 2000 (20%) |

The factory owner can change `maxContribution` and `maxMembers`, and can **pause** creating circles and joining them. Pausing never blocks `collect`, `payout`, `markDefault`, `claim` or any withdrawal, so money can always leave.

## Payouts and the pot

Invariant **I3**: every payout pot equals `activeMembers × C`. Before any ejection that's `n × C`. From the ejection round on, it's `(n − 1) × C`. Nothing else can reduce a pot.

A winner does **not** receive the whole pot in cash. Enough is held back to cover what they still owe. See [Safety](/docs/safety#withheld-collateral).
