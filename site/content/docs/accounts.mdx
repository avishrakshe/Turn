Your Turn account is unlocked with your face or fingerprint, the same way you unlock your phone. There's no password and no list of secret words to keep safe.

You never pay network fees: Turn's relayer sends your requests for you. The relayer can only pass on what you signed, so it can never take your money.

Auto-pay is allowed to do exactly one thing: pay this month's amount into your circle, once, until the circle ends. Anything else, like joining a new circle, bidding big, or withdrawing, asks for Face ID again.

## Passkey-derived accounts (mera)

Accounts are created with [mera](https://mera.category.xyz), which turns a WebAuthn passkey into an ordinary Ethereum key:

1. The passkey's **PRF extension** produces a secret that is unique to the credential and the site (`PRF(credential, rpId, salt)`).
2. That secret becomes a **BIP-39 seed**, and an HD derivation gives a secp256k1 key.
3. The same passkey gives **the same account on every device** where the passkey is available (for example, synced through iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager).

Turn derives one account per user, the primary account at HD index 0, behind a small `TurnKeyring` interface (`derive("primary")`). Adding a separate savings or recovery key later means mapping a new purpose to a new index, not a rewrite.

Creating an account takes **one prompt** (two on authenticators that don't support PRF at creation).

## EIP-7702 delegation to TurnAccount

The derived key is a plain externally owned account (EOA). On first use it signs an **EIP-7702 authorization** that delegates its code to `TurnAccount`, so the same address gains smart-account features:

- `execute(calls, nonce, deadline, sig)`: runs a batch of calls signed with EIP-712 by the account's own key. `value` is always 0.
- `grantPull` / `revokePull` / `pullContribution`: the contract-enforced auto-pay described below.
- Storage uses an ERC-7201 namespace (`turn.account.v1`), so it never collides with a future delegate.

<Callout title="Monad specifics we rely on">

On Monad, a delegated EOA's transaction reverts if it would take the account's MON balance below 10 MON. Turn users hold 0 MON and `TurnAccount` rejects any `value > 0`, so the balance never drops and the rule never triggers. `TurnAccount` also never uses `CREATE`, which reverts in a delegated context. Circles are created by the factory instead.

</Callout>

## The gasless relayer

The relayer submits transactions and pays gas. For the first action it sends a type-4 (EIP-7702) transaction that carries the authorization.

**What it can do:** refuse or delay your request, which is censorship.

**What it can't do:** change or invent a request. Every batch carries your EIP-712 signature over the calls, a nonce, a deadline and the chain ID, and `TurnAccount` checks it.

Guard rails on the relayer:

- Gas: `eth_estimateGas` plus 10%, a hard cap per call type, and a daily cap per account. Monad bills on the gas **limit**, so limits are kept tight.
- Rate limits per IP and per account.
- A target allowlist, checked both by the relayer and by `TurnAccount`: AUSD, the factory, factory-registered circles, and the account itself.

If the relayer ever censors you, every round function stays callable by anyone (see [Automation](/docs/automation)), and a member with MON can submit their own signed batch directly.

## Signing sessions: what needs Face ID

mera keeps signing keys **in memory only**. A session ends when the page closes, and `end()` zeroes the key. So nothing that must happen monthly can depend on a session. Auto-pay is enforced on-chain instead.

| Action | Face ID? | Enforced by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Create account and join first circle | **One prompt** | One mera ceremony signs the 7702 authorization and the batch `approve → join → grantPull` |
| Monthly contribution (auto-pay) | **None** | `PullGrant` in `TurnAccount` |
| Bid of 10% or less while the app is open | None | In-memory session, EIP-712 signature |
| Bid over 10%, join another circle, claim or withdraw, change payout destination, renew or revoke auto-pay | **Yes** | Fresh mera ceremony |
| Export recovery phrase | **Yes** | Settings only |
| After reload or on a new device | The first signing action asks | Reading needs no prompt |

### Exactly what auto-pay may do

`grantPull(circle, maxAmount, period, validUntil)` lets a circle pull a contribution without a signature, only when **all** of these hold:

- the caller is a circle registered by the factory
- `amount ≤ maxAmount` (the contribution C)
- at most one pull per round, at least `period − tolerance` apart
- `block.timestamp ≤ validUntil` (the circle's original end)
- the grant hasn't been revoked

Members who aren't delegated (for example, someone testing with MetaMask) fall back to a normal AUSD allowance and `transferFrom`.

## The stateless-device guarantee

Turn stores **nothing essential on your device**. Circles, payments, your display currency (`MemberProfileSet`), bids and scores all live on-chain and in the indexer. Clear your browser, or pick up a new phone, sign in with the same passkey, and everything comes back. The end-to-end test suite checks exactly this: clear storage, reload, restore.

<Callout tone="honest">

Signing in on a fresh device without a stored credential ID is still being verified with mera. Recovery-phrase export has no dedicated mera API, so Turn derives the phrase itself with `entropyToMnemonic`, behind a fresh Face ID prompt.

</Callout>
