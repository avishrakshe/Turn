<PlainWords>

Turn runs the savings committee your family already knows (a chit, a *committee*, a *kameti*) on a phone. Everyone puts in the same amount each month, and each month one person takes home the whole pot.

The difference is who holds the money. In a normal committee, one trusted person does. In Turn, a small program on a public blockchain does. It follows the rules everyone agreed to, it can't run off, and anyone can check what it did.

You never see any of that. You see Face ID, your own currency, and plain words.

</PlainWords>

<UnderTheHood />

## Why a blockchain at all

A savings circle is a trust problem, which is exactly what a blockchain is good at. We use one for four reasons.

| Need | What the chain gives us |
| --- | --- |
| **Nobody can take the pot** | The pot is held by the circle's own smart contract. It can only pay out according to its rules: to the round's winner, or back to members. No one holds a key that moves it anywhere else. |
| **Everyone can check** | Every payment, payout and missed month is a public event. Members don't have to trust the organiser's notebook. |
| **Money moves across borders instantly** | A member in Dubai and a member in Mumbai pay into the same pot in a dollar stablecoin. Settlement takes about a second on Monad, with no bank transfer fee taken from the pot. |
| **Your record is yours** | Paying on time builds a public [Turn Score](/docs/turn-score) that any app can read. It isn't locked in our database. |

## The pieces

  title="How the parts of Turn fit together"

- **Accounts.** Each member's account is derived from their passkey (Face ID or fingerprint) and upgraded with EIP-7702 to run `TurnAccount`. See [Accounts & passkeys](/docs/accounts).
- **Circles.** `CircleFactory` creates one `Circle` contract per group. It collects contributions, runs the auction, pays out, and covers missed payments. See [Circles](/docs/circles) and [Safety](/docs/safety).
- **Money.** Everything settles in AUSD, a US-dollar stablecoin. Local currencies are only a display layer. See [Money](/docs/money).
- **Automation.** Chainlink CRE calls the round functions on schedule. Anyone else can call them too. See [Automation](/docs/automation).
- **Data.** An Envio indexer turns contract events into the history, stats and scores the app shows. See [Data](/docs/data).

## Networks

Turn is being built on **Monad testnet** (chain ID `10143`) and will run a small, capped beta on **Monad mainnet** (chain ID `143`). Addresses for each network are listed on [Contracts](/docs/contracts), read from the deploy output, never typed by hand.
